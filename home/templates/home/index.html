LBPP S4

Owners: Team Rocket

Admin: Charu (this is not charu)

Format Outline

  • Pokemon Champion's Nat Dex Draft, with the typical Level 100 Pokemon and 508 EVs. For this, we will use the following Pokemon Showdown Server: https://champsnatdex.dedyn.io/
  • Z-Moves, Dynamax, and Terastalize are banned.
  • Teams will have 10-11 mons of up to 120 Points.
  • Teams may only draft 1 Pokemon that costs 2 or less.
  • Battles must be completed on a weekly basis with each week's deadline being Sunday 11:59 PM. Failure to meet the battle deadline will result in an automatic forfeit.

Complex & Pokémon Specific Rules

  • Shell Smash + Blastoise/Mega Blastoise
  • Battle Bond + Mega Greninja
  • Sing + Mega Raichu Y
  • Swords Dance + Mega Lopunny
  • Seismic Toss + Mega Kangaskhan
  • Shell Smash + Mega Barbaracle

Additional Draft and Free Agency Rules

  • The draft will be asynchronous, occurring over the span of a day or two. During the draft, each person will have up to 4 hours to make their pick. It is your prerogative to take all 2 hours, but it would be appreciated if you could pick earlier!! ^-^
  • Picks will be made via the discord bot Wiglett. Information on how to use the draft bot can be found here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r5wZwTkfels&feature=youtu.be
  • The draft will be paused between 11PM-7AM.
  • Each team has 5 free agents. The free agency period ends at the beginning of Week 5.
  • Teams will get 5 free agents and 5 inter-team trades. When dropping/picking mons, the transaction count is based on the number of mons picked up. Each team change will come into effect for the following week after the transaction is made.

Battle Rules and Standings

  • The following Smogon clauses are in effect:
    • Sleep Clause (certain exceptions exist, for those, refer to item 8; only one pokemon can sleep)
    • Evasion Clause
    • OHKO Clause
    • Endless Battle Clause
  • Moody, Sand Veil and Snow Cloak are banned.
  • Bright Powder, Lax Incense, King's Rock, Focus Band, Razor Fang and Quick Claw are banned.
  • Accupressure, Confuse Ray, Flatter, Supersonic, Swagger and Sweet Kiss are banned.
  • Battles that go to timer will be decided by who went to timer, the losing coach will have their score lowered to 0 (eg. if the game ends 2-2 then the result will be a 2-0).
  • Shed Tail is banned, Pursuit is banned, Last Respects is banned and Revival Blessing is banned. Baton Pass is allowed but cannot be used to pass stats or Substitute.
  • There is no Species Clause but you may only bring one of each of your drafted Pokémon to a game (eg you may not bring 2 Pikachus) but you can draft Ogerpon-H and Ogerpon-W! Why would you do that, though? ;)
  • If more than 1 Pokemon gets slept due to Effect Spore, Relic Song or Dire Claw then it will not result in a loss, otherwise sleep clause is in effect.
  • Battle timers can be turned on after 15 minutes by any player.
  • Each coach will have 8 battles.

    Tiebreakers:

    1. Head to head
    2. Differential
    3. Number of Losses
    4. Strength of Victory
