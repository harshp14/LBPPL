League Bois Pokemon Premier: Season 2

Draft Rules

  • The draft is randomised and in a snake format (1-15, 15-1 etc.)
  • Each coach will draft between 10 and 12 Pokémon.
  • Teams do not need to spend their full budget and can end their draft after 10 picks.
  • You only have 120 points to allocate for your team.
  • Teams may only draft 1 Pokémon that costs 2 or less.
  • Teams may only draft 1 Mega Pokémon, though you may choose not to draft one at all.
  • A coach will have 2 minutes maximum to make their pick; otherwise, they will be skipped over.
  • After the draft, coaches will have a one week grace period to make unlimited free agency transactions. These transactions do not count towards the free agency transaction total (see below).

Free Agency and Trades

  • All free agency transactions after the draft grace period must be recorded on the Free Agency Tracker tab and messaged on the LBPP transactions channel.
  • Teams will get 5 free agency transactions and 5 inter-team trades.
  • The trade and transaction deadline is after the last battle of Week 5 concludes. After that battle is resolved, teams will no longer be able to change their team in any manner. A team can also not make any changes after they play their Week 6 battle. In addition, a team may not make changes if they've already played 5 matches, to prevent players from getting through their matches early and having the opportunity to change their team for more battles.
  • When dropping/picking Pokémon, the trade/transaction count is based on the number of Pokémon picked up.
  • Trades should only include Pokémon-based bargaining chips.
  • Regardless of when a trade or transaction is made, teams need to ensure that after all immediate transactions resolve, their roster still adheres to all draft rules. Otherwise, the trade will be nullified. For example, if a trade would put your team over the 120 point cap, you must declare a Pokémon you are dropping to adhere to the cap simultaneously with the trade.

Battle Rules

  • Find your opponent's username, chat with them, then paste the challenge string with the National Dex format, point changes, and clauses (see the Rules tab of the S2 sheet for the exact challenge text).
  • Forfeiting during a match is strictly prohibited as it is poor sportsmanship. A forfeit counts as a 3-0 win/loss (or more, depending on when it occurs); a forfeit during the final week is a 6-0 loss.
  • Battles will be played at Level 100, with all Pokémon being Level 100. 6 Pokémon must be brought to the battle.
  • Ghosting is prohibited; failure to comply is met with penalties, automatic losses, or expulsion from the league.
  • Megas: if a Pokémon is drafted as a Mega, it MUST hold its Mega Stone in battle, but does NOT have to Mega Evolve.
  • Z-Moves, Dynamax, and Terastallization are banned.
  • Automatic forfeits can be given if rules are broken, especially if it has a significant effect on the battle.

Clauses and Bans

  • Species Clause, Sleep Clause, Evasion Clause, OHKO Clause, Moody Clause, Endless Battle Clause and Baton Pass Clause are all in effect.
  • If more than 1 Pokémon gets slept due to Effect Spore, Relic Song or Dire Claw then it will not result in a loss; otherwise Sleep Clause is in effect. An inadvertent Baton Passed condition (e.g. an enemy Shadow Ball lowering Special Defense) will not result in a loss; otherwise Baton Pass Clause is in effect.
  • Banned moves: Hidden Power, Last Respects, Accupressure, Confuse Ray, Flatter, Supersonic, Swagger, Sweet Kiss and Shed Tail.
  • Banned items: Bright Powder, Lax Incense, King's Rock, Focus Band, Razor Fang and Quick Claw.
  • Complex bans: Take Heart Manaphy, Shell Smash Blastoise/Mega Blastoise, and Power Construct on Zygarde.
  • Battles that go to timer will be decided by who went to timer; the losing coach will have their score lowered to 0 (eg. if the game ends 2-2 then the result will be a 2-0).

Standings and Playoffs

  • The league is split into 3 divisions of 5 teams, with a similar distribution of player strength; standings are decided within conference.
  • Each coach has 10 battles and 1 bye: 4 in-division battles (higher priority on tiebreakers) and 6 out-of-division battles, spread across 5 strength tiers agreed to by a majority of coaches. Divisions are determined after the draft and grace period conclude.
  • Tiebreakers (in order): Division Wins, Head-to-Head, Differential, Number of Losses. For tiebreakers between teams in different divisions, division wins are ignored. Further tiebreakers, if necessary, are determined by Bo3 battle factory games.
  • The knockouts are a 12-team single elimination. The 1st place team in each division plus the best 2nd place team get a first-round bye; the bottom team in each division is eliminated.
