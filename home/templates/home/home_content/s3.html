LBPP S3

Owners: Team Rocket

Admin: Charu

Format Outline

  • Paldea Dex Draft with High-Tier Teras and Low-Tier Ubers!
  • Teams will have 10 mons of up to 125 Points, and up to 2 Tera captains of up to 26 Tera Points.
  • Tera Captains can Tera to any type! However, before a battle, Tera previews must be enabled. In other words, your opponent will know what your Tera type is during the battle.
  • If a mon is designated as a Tera Captain, it costs more, AND that point total will also count against the Tera budget. A team must adhere to both its normal and Tera budgets. E.g., if you draft a Kingambit normally, it is 14 points, and you would have 111 points left and 26 Tera points. If you draft Kingambit as a Tera, it will cost 22 and you will be left with 103 points for the draft and 4 points for your remaining Teras. A Pokémon drafted as a Tera does NOT have to Terastallize in the battle.
  • Teams may only draft 1 Pokémon that costs 2 or less.
  • Battles must be completed on a weekly basis with each week's deadline being Sunday 11:59 PM. Failure to meet the battle deadline will result in an automatic forfeit.

Complex & Pokémon Specific Rules

  • Shell Smash + Blastoise
  • Take Heart + Manaphy

Additional Draft and Free Agency Rules

  • The draft will be asynchronous, occurring over the span of a day or two. During the draft, each person will have up to 2 hours to make their pick. It is your prerogative to take all 2 hours, but it would be appreciated if you could pick earlier!! ^-^
  • Picks will be made via the discord bot Wiglett. Information on how to use the draft bot can be found here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r5wZwTkfels&feature=youtu.be
  • When you want to pick a mon as a Tera, please select the Tera tab on the draft board. You will need to keep track of your pointage, as the Discord bot can't properly account for Teras.
  • The draft will be paused between 12AM-9AM.
  • Each team has 5 free agents. The free agency period ends EOD February 8th.
  • Teams will get 5 free agents and 5 inter-team trades. When dropping/picking mons, the transaction count is based on the number of mons picked up.
  • Teams will be allowed to reallocate Teras between Pokémon on the team already 3 times. If Tera is dropped from a mon, it cannot be reassigned that ability.
  • FA and inter-team trades do not count until the following week. (Note: FA changes for the Week 1 battle will count if made 7 days before scheduled battle time.)
  • Regardless of when a trade or transaction is made, teams need to ensure that after all immediate transactions resolve, their roster still adheres to all draft rules. Otherwise, the trade will be nullified.
  • Trades should only include Pokémon-based bargaining chips.

Battle Rules and Playoffs

  • We will use Tera preview; i.e., you will know what your opponent can Tera their captains into.
  • Battles will be played at Level 100, with all Pokémon being Level 100. 6 Pokémon must be brought to the battle.
  • Battles must occur each week, otherwise ff.
  • The following Smogon clauses are in effect:
    • Sleep Clause (certain exceptions exist, for those, refer to item 8; only one pokemon can sleep)
    • Evasion Clause
    • OHKO Clause
    • Endless Battle Clause
  • Moody, Sand Veil and Snow Cloak are banned.
  • Bright Powder, Lax Incense, King's Rock, Focus Band, Razor Fang and Quick Claw are banned.
  • Accupressure, Confuse Ray, Flatter, Supersonic, Swagger and Sweet Kiss are banned.
  • Battles that go to timer will be decided by who went to timer, the losing coach will have their score lowered to 0 (eg. if the game ends 2-2 then the result will be a 2-0).
  • Shed Tail is banned, Last Respects is banned and Revival Blessing is banned. Baton Pass is allowed but cannot be used to pass stats or Substitute.
  • There is no Species Clause but you may only bring one of each of your drafted Pokémon to a game (eg you may not bring 2 Pikachus) but you can draft Ogerpon-H and Ogerpon-W! Why would you do that, though? ;)
  • If more than 1 Pokemon gets slept due to Effect Spore, Relic Song or Dire Claw then it will not result in a loss, otherwise sleep clause is in effect.
  • Battle timers can be turned on after 15 minutes, regardless.
  • Each coach will have 11 battles.

    Tiebreakers:

    1. Head to head
    2. Differential
    3. Number of Losses
    4. Strength of Victory
  • The Knockouts will be 8-team single elimination. The bottom 4 teams will be eliminated. The 1st-4th place teams will be allowed to pick their playoff round 1 opponents from among the 5th-8th place teams.
